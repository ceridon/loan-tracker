? What are principal and interest

Principal = the money you actually borrowed (your debt).
Interest = the extra you pay the lender for borrowing.

At the start of a loan, most of your monthly goes to interest. That is why paying extra on the principal saves you so much.

Extra payment

Any extra money you pay straight to the principal, like your 13th month pay.

? Before you pay extra

Tell the lender clearly it is "applied to principal". If not, it may be counted only as an advance for your next monthly. For Pag-IBIG, you usually have to do this at their branch. Ask about any penalty, especially for bank loans.

? How this is computed

Pag-IBIG checks your capacity to pay: your monthly should not go over 35% of your gross monthly income. Most banks use a similar limit.

Years to pay: up to 30 years for Pag-IBIG (20 to 25 for most banks), and the loan should be fully paid by age 70.
